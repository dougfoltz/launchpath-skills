# My Launch Roadmap

## Tools

- `get_plant_overview` — current stage, days to launch, progress by area, what just opened up, what to work on now. Start here.
- `list_tasks` — pull the full task list (optionally filtered by area) to build a category-by-category completion breakdown.
- `my_tasks` — tasks specifically assigned to this user, behind-pace first, if the plant has more than one team member.

## Workflow

Lead with `get_plant_overview`'s framing (stage, days to launch, overall %). Then break down by area using `list_tasks` — show completion % per roadmap area (Leadership, Finances, Facilities, etc.) so the planter can see which categories are ahead and which are lagging, not just one aggregate number. If the plant has a team, layer in `my_tasks` to show what's specifically on this person's plate versus the team's.

## Artifact

A vertical stage-flow diagram: stages completed (checkmark), current stage (highlighted), remaining stages ahead (grayed). Beside it, a category breakdown bar or grid showing each roadmap area as complete (green), in-progress (blue), or not-started (gray).

## What not to do

- Don't collapse this into a single "% complete" number — the category breakdown is the useful part; a planter already knows their rough percentage from the app.
- Don't call `get_task` on individual tasks here — that's `task-deep-dive`.
